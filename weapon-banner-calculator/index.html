How it works

The weapon 5-star rate is 0.7% until pity 63, then rises steeply to a guaranteed drop at 77. A 5-star is a featured weapon 75% of the time, and your chosen one half of that (37.5%). One Fate Point guarantees the chosen weapon on your next 5-star, so the worst case is 154 wishes from zero.

Questions

What is the worst case for a signature weapon?

Two hard pities, 154 wishes or 24,640 Primogems, if you miss the chosen weapon on the first 5-star.

When does Epitomized Path reset?

Fate Points reset when you obtain the chosen weapon or change your path.
