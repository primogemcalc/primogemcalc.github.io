Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my chance of a 5-star?

Open the pity calculator, enter your pity, guarantee status and wishes, and it returns your exact odds.

How many Primogems do I need for a guaranteed character?

Up to 180 wishes, or 28,800 Primogems, from zero pity with no guarantee.

What is soft pity?

The wish where your 5-star rate starts rising: about 74 for characters and 63 for weapons.

Are these calculators official?

No. They are fan-made and use commonly published rates.
