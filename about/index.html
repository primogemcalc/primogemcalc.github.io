About PrimogemCalc

Last updated November 23, 2026

What we do

PrimogemCalc is a set of free, fan-made calculators that help Genshin Impact players plan their Primogems and wishes. Every tool runs in your browser.

How the numbers work

The pity and weapon calculators step through every wish using commonly published rates: a 0.6% base 5-star rate with soft pity at 74 and hard pity at 90 for characters, and 0.7% with soft pity at 63 and hard pity at 77 for weapons.

Our goal

Clear odds and honest costs, so you can decide when to pull and when to save. See our disclaimer for the limits of these estimates, or contact us with corrections.
