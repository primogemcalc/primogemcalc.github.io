Genshin Pity & 50/50 Probability Calculator

Enter your pity and how many wishes you can afford to see your odds.

How it works

The calculator steps through every wish. The 5-star rate is 0.6% until pity 74, then climbs about 6% per pull to a hard pity of 90. A 5-star is featured 50% of the time, or always if your last 5-star was not featured. Capturing Radiance raises the first-try odds to roughly 55%. Each wish costs 160 Primogems.

Questions

How many wishes is hard pity?

Character banner hard pity is 90 wishes, with soft pity starting around wish 74.

Does pity carry over?

Pity carries over between character banners, and so does the guarantee after losing a 50/50.
