How it works

Every option is converted to Primogems and ranked by Primogems per US dollar. First-time 2x doubles the base Crystals of each pack once per pack. The Welkin Moon gives 300 Crystals plus 90 Primogems daily for 30 days. Battle Pass value counts the 4 Intertwined Fates at 160 Primogems each.

Questions

Is the Welkin Moon worth it?

It usually gives the best Primogem value per dollar after the first-time bonuses.

Are the prices exact?

They are USD list prices converted at rough rates, so regional store prices may differ.
